## Instructions to Authors

- Instructions to authors are encapsulated in angle brackets like <...> and are italicized in the source template. Delete all such instructions before publication.
- Use standard English capitalization. Proper nouns and names are uppercase; do not use uppercase for emphasis.
- Review editing conventions before drafting: http://wiki.ihe.net/index.php?title=Writing_Technical_Frameworks_and_Supplements
- This template is for new profiles or significant changes (e.g., adding formal options). Simple changes should use the Change Proposal (CP) process.
- All sections are required. Do not delete sections or change outline numbering. If no relevant content, write "Section not applicable" and keep numbering.
- Templates for Volumes 1-4 are included. Volumes 1-3 are developed for Public Comment and Trial Implementation. Volume 4 (National Extensions) is usually later and should generally be avoided for new profiles.
